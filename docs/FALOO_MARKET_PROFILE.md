# Faloo market profile for TruyenCity

Captured 2026-08-14. This is a product grammar, not a prose imitation guide.
TruyenCity may learn packaging, pace and reward density; it must not copy an existing
title, character, franchise, setting or scene.

## TruyenCity contract

### Topic

Each concept combines:

1. A broad, instantly legible arena: global awakening, lords, apocalypse, cultivation,
   rebirth, entertainment, games, dual worlds, appraisal or competitive career.
2. A concrete protagonist position: discarded class, ruined heir, indebted family,
   last-ranked territory, unknown creator.
3. One advantage that produces visible scenes and can scale: infinite evolution,
   multiplier, simulation, ranking broadcast, summon, foreknowledge, conversion,
   appraisal or cross-world trade.

A quiet profession, cozy routine or technical process may supply texture, but it cannot
be the primary reader fantasy. The concept needs competition, power, status, wealth,
recognition or territory at a scale the reader can anticipate from the package.

The factory materializes this as a structured `marketBlueprint`, not prompt-only advice:

- `familiarArena` + `noveltyCollision`: a legible category with one fresh collision;
- `protagonistStartingPosition` + `coreAdvantage`: the starting disadvantage and the
  repeatable advantage that changes it;
- `comparisonEngine`: the market, ranking, exam, battle, auction, territory or other
  causal channel that makes gains visible to interested actors;
- `worldConflictEngine`: scarce resources, access rules and power tiers that keep creating
  conflict after the first antagonist loses;
- exact payoff checkpoints at chapters 1, 3, 5, 7 and 10;
- a 6–8-step scale ladder that changes arena, status prize, opposition class and how the
  advantage evolves.

### World and long setup

The world must create decisions and competition. A launch requires at least four meaningful
geographies, four institutions with different interests and two operating systems, including
both value creation and a way value/status is distributed or compared. Decorative lore does
not count.

The 800–1,200-chapter spine must have at least three independent progression tracks and six
promises. Every stage after the first seeds at least two concrete expansions. Moving to a new
stage must change the opponent class and the use of the core advantage, not merely rename the
same workshop, village or monster tier.

The first 20–30 chapter arc is split into mini-cycles and must cash the title promise early.
It cannot spend the whole arc repairing equipment, gathering materials, learning the world or
fighting one antagonist through repeated variations of the same exchange.

### Title and synopsis

- Title: 7–26 Vietnamese words; preferred form is
  `ARENA/IDENTITY: PROTAGONIST + ADVANTAGE + PAYOFF`.
- Say the fantasy directly. Avoid abstract occupations, static object names and literary
  mystery when the premise itself is the sales asset.
- Synopsis order: protagonist's immediate pressure → unique advantage → first payoff →
  larger escalation. Do not lead with world history or atmosphere.

### Opening and serial pace

- First ~200 words: pressure, opportunity or consequential action.
- Chapter 1: advantage activates and earns a visible first payoff.
- Chapter 2: the effect, witness set or reward expands.
- Chapter 3: a larger opponent, arena or target becomes concrete.
- Every chapter: one felt value movement. Every three chapters: a visible rise in scope
  or position. Every five chapters: a medium payoff and a new expectation.
- Technical detail stays only when it creates a decision, cost, risk or competitive edge.
  It must not turn the chapter into a manual.

## Production audit that triggered the correction

The five active production novels had sound causal ledgers but weak market packaging:

| Current title | Chapter | Primary drift |
|---|---:|---|
| Đại Địa Quy Hoạch Sư | 48 | Abstract occupation title; long mechanism explanation; muted wins. |
| Bác Sĩ Tàu Thuyền: Kỷ Nguyên Động Cơ | 68 | Repeated microscopic repair workflow; profession before fantasy. |
| Trùng Sinh Thập Niên 80: Ông Trùm Hải Sản | 62 | Best package of the group, but chapters still over-index on process. |
| Giao Kèo Giả Kim | 50 | High-concept mechanism hidden behind a short literary title. |
| Trình Độ Câu Mực Biển Khơi | 26 | Static title and repetitive catch–sell–repay loop; currently plan-blocked. |

The correction therefore applies at four layers: portfolio allocation, Concept Lab
packaging, opening simulation and chapter-level planning/writing/review. Causal,
resource, continuity and opposition-agency validators remain fail-closed.
